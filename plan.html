Optional Tokyo guide notes
  • Rail: Tokyo Metro’s subway guide and video. At Kita-Senju, show staff: この電車は春日部に止まりますか？ (“Does this train stop at Kasukabe?”). Use each person’s physical Suica and top up in increments based on remaining local fares. The paid Nozomi fare and AONIYOSHI supplement are separate.
  • Stamps: ask at JR Ueno, Akihabara or Tokyo Station as you pass through. Asakusa’s visitor centre may provide a dedicated sheet for a layered stamp. Sensō-ji’s goshuin traditionally goes in a separate goshuin book. JR East stamp reference.
  • Nearby shopping: OK Asakusa for everyday groceries, Gyomu Super for bulk, Don Quijote for late snacks and souvenirs. Asakusa UNIQLO is a separate branch from the Ginza flagship. HINOYA in Ueno is an optional denim stop when convenient.
  • Water: mymizu lists public refill points and partner locations. Check pin details and partner hours. Near Senzoku, look for the Senzoku Park drinking tap or check the refill at MUJI Asakusa ROX. Cold Tokyo tap water is drinkable.
  • Food labels: use Lens on 原材料名 and check separate sauce or broth packets. Watch for かつお/鰹 (bonito), 煮干し (dried fish), 魚介 (seafood), 肉エキス (meat extract), だし (stock) and 卵 (egg). Food and dietary guide.
