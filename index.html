Vegetarian food (no egg, dairy OK)

Show this to staff:
私はベジタリアンです。肉・魚・魚介類・卵、かつお節、魚のだし、肉エキス、ゼラチンは食べられません。乳製品（牛乳・チーズ・バター）と、昆布・しいたけのだしは大丈夫です。

"I'm vegetarian. I can't eat meat, fish, seafood, eggs, bonito flakes, fish stock, meat extract or gelatin. Dairy and kombu or shiitake stock are fine."

Words to check on labels
  • 卵 egg · かつお/鰹 bonito · だし/出汁 stock
  • 魚介エキス seafood extract · 肉/チキンエキス meat/chicken extract
  • ゼラチン gelatin · 動物性油脂 animal fat · 乳 milk (OK)
Convenience-store backups
  • Safe: salt onigiri 塩むすび ✅, edamame, nuts, milk, yogurt, cheese. 7-Eleven curry labelled 動物性原材料不使用 ("no animal ingredients").
  • Avoid: umeboshi onigiri (FamilyMart's contains sardine), mayonnaise sandwiches, kombu or inari onigiri unless the label is clear.
  • Onigiri ¥178–235. Bento for him ¥500–800.
Ask before ordering
  • Tempura batter often contains egg.
  • Noodles at restaurants that also serve meat.
  • Fried rice: say "no egg" (卵なし).
  • Pokémon Café: everything except plain drinks contains egg ✅.

Apps: HappyCow, Vegewel, Japan VegeMap. Fully vegan places (T's Tantan, Shojin Ramen Sai, Uno, Itadakizen, Shigetsu, Playpen Friends, Paprika) are egg-free.
